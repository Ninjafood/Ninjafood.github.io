Clad — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version: the developer of Clad collects nothing. Clad has no accounts and no server of its own. To design parts it uses an AI model that you choose. With Apple Intelligence or another on-device model, nothing leaves your device. With a cloud model, what you ask for is sent from your device directly to that model's provider.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer does not collect, store, receive, sell, or share any personal information, content, or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What is sent to a cloud AI provider, if you choose one

When you send a request with a cloud model selected, Clad sends the following from your device directly to the provider selected in Settings, using your own API key:

  • the text of your request and the conversation about the current design;
  • the design's program code and, when enabled, pictures of the part as shown in the app;
  • any photo or sketch you choose to attach to a request.

The providers Clad can connect to are Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google (Gemini) and OpenRouter. This data does not pass through the developer. How a provider handles it is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy, under your account with them.

Apple Intelligence and other on-device models

If you choose Apple Intelligence, requests are handled on your device by the model built into it, and Clad sends them nowhere. If you choose a downloadable on-device model, requests are also processed on your device; that model's files are downloaded once from a public model host.

Your own server

If you point Clad at a model server on your own network, requests go only to that server.

What stays on your device

Your designs, your settings, and any API keys you enter are stored on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to attach a photo to a request. An attached photo is sent to your chosen AI provider as described above, and nowhere else.

Children

Consistent with the above, the developer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
