Frequently asked

  • Why does nothing happen when I send a request? Clad needs an AI model to design parts. Open Settings and either add your own API key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, point it at a model server on your own network, or choose an on-device model.
  • Does Clad include an AI subscription? No. Requests are billed by the AI provider you choose, on your own account with them.
  • What works without a model? The starter part, the Params sliders, the Print checks, saved designs and export.
  • How do I change one spot on a part? Tap a face, edge or corner to select it, then describe the change. Tap it again to deselect.
  • How do I get my part to a printer? Tap Export and choose STL or STEP, then send the file wherever you like from the share sheet.
  • Where are my designs kept? On your device, in the Designs list. Deleting the app deletes them.

Privacy

Clad's developer collects no data. Requests go from your device directly to the AI provider you choose. Read the full privacy policy.
