Frequently asked

  • Which AI does Clad use? On an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence switched on, Clad starts on the model built into the device: no key, no network. It builds Clad's ready-made parts (tray, box with lid, knob, wall hook, coaster and more) and changes their sizes. To design anything else, open Settings and add your own API key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, or point it at a model server on your own network.
  • Apple Intelligence says it cannot do something. The built-in model cannot add or reshape features, and it cannot see photos or sketches. Choose a cloud model or a local server in Settings for those.
  • Does Clad include an AI subscription? No. Apple Intelligence is free on devices that have it. Requests to a cloud provider are billed by that provider, on your own account with them.
  • What works without a model? The starter part, the Params sliders, the Print checks, saved designs and export.
  • How do I change one spot on a part? Tap a face, edge or corner to select it, then describe the change. Tap it again to deselect.
  • How do I get my part to a printer? Tap Export and choose STL or STEP, then send the file wherever you like from the share sheet.
  • Where are my designs kept? On your device, in the Designs list. Deleting the app deletes them.

Privacy

Clad's developer collects no data. With Apple Intelligence nothing leaves your device; with a cloud model, requests go from your device directly to the provider you choose. Read the full privacy policy.
